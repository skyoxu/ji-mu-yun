- (decision) Adopt original capability draft sections 15, 16, and 18.5 as normative downstream companion; preserve concrete protocol details without duplicating wrapper ceremony.
- (capability) CAP-1 VDD extracts complete source-bound obligations; success: active obligations have stable IDs, refs, types, dependencies, and hard-uncovered is zero before plan-ready.
- (capability) CAP-2 Acceptance compiler creates observable, discriminating, attributable, implementable, testable, stage-correct, non-self-judging Acceptance; success: every active obligation has at least one valid Acceptance and RED intent.
- (capability) CAP-3 VDD builds sound-and-complete many-to-many coverage and deterministic slices; success: no orphan requirement, obligation, Acceptance, slice, or RED intent and write-set feasibility passes.
- (capability) CAP-4 Quick Dev provides recommendation-only preflight and explicit planned/observed/recovered/invalid run states; success: diagnostics have no execution side effects and planned-only never satisfies completion.
- (capability) CAP-5 Quick Dev materializes and executes real RED from VDD intent, deriving failure classification from process observations; success: expected-red requires executions at least one and rejects harness, repo-noise, timeout, and unexpected-green.
- (capability) CAP-6 GREEN and REFACTOR reuse the RED selector identity and enforce production write-set boundaries; success: same target, fixture, assertions, and cwd are re-executed and out-of-scope changes fail closed.
- (capability) CAP-7 Slice-ready and terminal predicates validate real assertion edges, explicit lineage, hashes, exact coverage, regressions, and mutation; success: only deterministic evidence-derived implementation-complete is emitted.
- (capability) CAP-8 Selective replay and recovery invalidate only semantically affected observations and never infer predecessors from glob, mtime, or latest-success scans; success: explicit run-local lineage restores valid observations and rejects invalid ones.
- (capability) CAP-9 Detached fixtures and independent judges verify false-green resistance and generalization; success: positive, negative, mutation, 8-25 replay, and one unseen medium task pass without plan-specific exceptions.
- (constraint) VDD declares intent only; Quick Dev owns descriptor materialization and execution; terminal owns implementation-complete only; external semantic acceptance and maintainer commit/release decisions remain outside Quick Dev.
- (constraint) All execution commands use argument arrays, shell=false, contained cwd, explicit target/fixture identity, and declared write sets; model workers cannot write plan-ready, pass, or observed evidence.
- (constraint) Development governance defaults off while authentication, TDD, semantic predicates, write-set safety, and runtime safety remain enabled; test and production may enable governance without weakening TDD truth.
- (non-goal) Do not restore Taskmaster MCP, game-domain pipelines, Chapter 6.7/6.8 reviewer governance, distributed orchestration, CI/CD, or commit/PR/release authority.
- (assumption) Historical v1 plans require read-only compatibility projection and repair recommendations rather than universal automatic migration.
- (question) Spec/Architecture must freeze the exact obligation-Acceptance normalization, split/merge thresholds, assertion-edge schema, deterministic validator boundary, v1 adapter, unexpected-green proof, 60-minute task size, profile differences, invalidation boundary, and detached-fixture independence before implementation.
